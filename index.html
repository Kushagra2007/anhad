ANHAD ’26 is GCET’s flagship three-day celebration of literature, poetry, journalism, oral storytelling, and live music — bringing together student writers, debaters, and readers across Delhi NCR.

Three Days. Three Distinct Moods.

Day 1 · 26 Nov
Aarambh — The Beginning of Stories. Kitab Ghar book fair, authors, Turncoat throwdowns, Kavi Sammelan & fresh literary voices.
Day 2 · 27 Nov
Samvaad — The Art of Conversation. Fourth Pillar of Democracy panel, Bhagat Singh archival exhibit, Dastangoi & Sufi Night.
Day 3 · 28 Nov
Naad — The Rhythm of Celebration. Conscious living reading circle, Open Mic, Debate Finals, freshers’ artistry & closing concert.
“Every age has its Kurukshetra, and every awakening begins with a word.” — ANHAD ’26 · Where Ideas Find Expression

COMPETITIONS 3

Curated Showcases

Exhibits, Panels & Mehfils

Bhagat Singh: The Revolutionary Pen

Archival Exhibit · Day 2 (10:30 AM – 5:00 PM) · Heritage Gallery

Beyond rebellion, Bhagat Singh was a voracious reader, essayist, and thinker who believed revolution begins with ideas. Explore annotated readings of Why I Am an Atheist, prison notebooks, and letters.

Kitab Ghar — Courtyard Book Fair & Swap

All Three Days · Central Courtyard

Independent Hindi, Urdu, and English publishers, campus book swaps, and quiet reading benches. “किताबें झाँकती हैं बंद अलमारी के शीशों से, बड़ी हसरत से तकती हैं।” — Bashir Badr

Fourth Pillar of Democracy

Journalism & Media Dialogue · Day 2 · Main Auditorium

Long-form reporters, editors, and student journalists unpack investigative reporting, campus press independence, and verification in the age of noise.

Dastangoi & Evening Sufi Night

Live Oral & Musical Recitals · Day 2 Dusk · Amphitheatre & Main Lawn

Traditional 16th-century Urdu storytelling followed by an acoustic Sufi qawwali evening. “गुलों में रंग भरे बाद-ए-नौ-बहार चले, चले भी आओ कि गुलशन का कारोबार चले।” — Faiz

Confirmed Stage Formats
