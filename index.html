A Festival of Words

❦

Anhad is the premier literature festival of Galgotias College of Engineering & Technology — an open book where stories, poems, ideas, and voices share one stage. Whether you read, write, recite, or simply love to listen, there is a chapter here with your name on it.

The Revolutionary Pen

❦

भगत सिंह

The Revolutionary Who Believed in the Power of Ideas

Bhagat Singh was one of India's most influential revolutionary thinkers, remembered for his courage, intellectual thinking, and unwavering commitment to independence. Beyond his revolutionary activities, he was an insatiable reader, writer, and thinker who believed that true revolution began with ideas and awareness.

“Before the slogan echoed across the streets, it was forged in prison cells surrounded by books, essays, and an unbreakable self-belief.”

His famous treatise Why I Am an Atheist and his smuggled jail diaries reflected his rational mind. Because the sharpest weapon against any empire is a mind that dares to read and a pen that dares to write.

“किताबें झाँकती हैं बंद अलमारी के शीशों से,
बड़ी हसरत से तकती हैं।”

— बशीर बद्र / गुलज़ार · Kitab Ghar Exhibit

Patrons & Inquiries · जिज्ञासा

Questions, Answered

❦
How do I register as an attendee?

Registration for festival visitors and literature lovers is hosted on Luma. Simply reserve your free entry pass via the link in Chapter IV or on the Cover.

Who is eligible for competitions?

All undergraduate and postgraduate students from any recognized college or university across India are welcome. Register on Unstop.

Is there an entry fee for visitors?

General entry to attendee keynotes, book fairs, and courtyard performances is free upon pre-registration. Specific competition slots follow Unstop guidelines.

How can brands or publishers partner with ANHAD?

We welcome publishers, book stores, literary guilds, food partners, and corporate sponsors. Write to the Student Council at studentcouncil.gcet@galgotiascollege.edu.
