Preface · आमुख

A festival of words

Anhad is the literature fest of Galgotias College of Engineering & Technology — an open book where stories, poems, ideas and voices share one stage. Whether you read, write, recite or simply love to listen, there is a chapter here with your name on it.

✦ Interactive · Invoke a Literary Rasa नवरस काव्य

“हद चले सो मानवा, बेहद चले सो साध। हद बेहद दोनों तजे, ताकर मता अगाध॥”

“Within limits walks the mortal; beyond limits walks the seeker. In the boundless unstruck sound (Anhad) lies infinite wisdom.” — Kabir
